# business — what we sell and to whom

One job: hold the offer, the avatar, and the numbers the business is judged on.

## What lives here
- `offer.md` — what we sell, the price, the promise, the guarantee. One file. If pricing changes, change it here and nowhere else.
- `avatar.md` — who buys: their situation, what they've tried, what they say in their own words.
- `numbers.md` — the three to five numbers you check weekly (leads, calls, closes, revenue, churn).

## Inputs
- Reference (every task): `../_shared/business.md`, `../_shared/voice.md`

Do NOT load: `clients/`, `content/`. Those read from here, not the other way round.

## Rules
- The offer has one file. Two versions of the offer means neither is real.
- Avatar quotes are verbatim from real people (calls, DMs, surveys). No invented personas.

## Human check
Read `offer.md` out loud once a month. If it doesn't match what you say on sales calls, fix the file.
