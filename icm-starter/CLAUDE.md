This workspace is built on ICM: folders carry the job, hierarchy carries context, files carry state. The structure is the documentation. If something needs explaining, it goes in that folder's `CONTEXT.md`, not in your head and not here.

**Read `_shared/business.md` and `_shared/voice.md` before writing anything for me.**

## Where things live

| Folder | Job |
|---|---|
| `business/` | what we sell, who we sell to, the offer, the numbers we care about |
| `content/` | everything we publish: ideas, scripts, posts, results |
| `clients/` | the people paying us: onboarding, delivery, wins, renewals |
| `money/` | invoices, expenses, monthly numbers |
| `ops/` | tools, logins (never the secrets themselves), routines, the inbox for unplaced files |
| `_shared/` | facts and rules that never change per task |
| `_templates/` | blank starters. New work is a copy, not a blank page |

## Route by what I ask

| If I ask about… | Go to | Then stop |
|---|---|---|
| the offer, pricing, my audience | `business/CONTEXT.md` | answer from what's there |
| a post, a script, a video idea | `content/CONTEXT.md` | draft in my voice |
| a specific client | `clients/CONTEXT.md` → their folder | never mix two clients' files |
| what we made or spent | `money/CONTEXT.md` | numbers only from the files, never estimated |
| a tool, a routine, a login | `ops/CONTEXT.md` | |

## The rules

1. **Read the folder's `CONTEXT.md` before opening anything else in it.** One file first, not fifty.
2. **Don't load what you weren't sent to.** If the answer is in `content/`, stay out of `clients/`.
3. **One home per thing.** If a file could live in two places, it goes in one and the other links to it.
4. **Never invent a number.** If it isn't in `money/` or `business/`, say so.
5. **A move and its repointing are one change.** Move a file, fix every link to it, then stop.
6. **Retire loudly.** When something is replaced, leave a one-line note saying what replaced it.
